Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Alarm Clock Alex mobile application for iOS. The app is designed with absolute privacy in mind and operates as a fully standalone local utility.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Microphone Access: The app requests microphone access solely to allow you to record your personal voice reminders (morning notes). Audio recording happens strictly under your manual control.

Audio Storage: All recorded voice notes and imported custom alarm sounds are stored strictly locally on your device within the secure application sandbox or shared App Group container.

2. Data Transmission and Third Parties

The app contains no network code, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking scripts. No data, audio files, or configuration settings ever leave your iPhone. No information is collected, processed, or shared with third parties or the developer.

3. Revoking Permissions and Deletion

You can delete any voice reminder directly inside the app using the hold-to-delete gesture. You can fully revoke microphone permissions at any time via iOS System Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Microphone.

4. Support Contact

If you have any questions, please contact the developer via the official repository page.
